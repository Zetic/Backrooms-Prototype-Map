Actual floor heights and connection footprints. Inspect data for the future 3D generator.

Carrying a stair on up is how a raised branch is entered in the world (claims.js): a two-storey house's stairwell climbs one more flight to a landing above its roof, run out to the edge of its lot with a door there. Connection zones are where a template can climb or descend. Each template prefers some types; a type that does not fit falls through to the next, down to the ladder every template has. A connected variant adds a real landing, its cutouts and a reservation that follows the slope. A journey stacks such climbs: different templates, one above another, from one band's floor to the next band's 16 m up, with a floor to cross between each climb.
