Room-first houses (test bed)

A separate generator, apart from the template engines: every room's floor is reserved first at its own size, then one wall builder puts the walls in the space between rooms. Interior walls are always 0.15 m and outer walls 0.3 m, outside the rooms. Sizes shown are clear floor. The passage houses are built along the way through them: a route of rooms between a primary connection (front-door role) and secondary connections leading out of the house, with the rest of the home off its sides.
